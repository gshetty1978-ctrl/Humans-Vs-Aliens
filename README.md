# Humans vs Aliens

A pixel-art lane-defense game: place humans on the grid, spend energy, and stop the alien invasion across 12 worlds and 60 levels with 4 world-ending bosses.

**Play it:** https://cricketlover.itch.io/humans-vs-aliens

- Also playable at https://humans-vs-aliens.onrender.com (add it to your phone's Home Screen to play offline).
- 8 humans, reactor, firewall and dynamite, 70+ aliens and bosses, upgrades, shop, achievements, sandbox mode.
- Controls: click or drag a card onto the grid, click falling energy, `1-6` pick a human, `R`/`F`/`D` reactor/firewall/dynamite, `C` collect all, `U` Max ultimate, `P`/`Esc` pause.
